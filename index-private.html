Lake District / Windermere: July 24–28

Base Lakes Hotel and Spa, Kendal Road, Bowness, Windermere, LA23 3EP.

Jul 24 — Arrival, Spa, Lakefront Evening

  • Train from Cambridge to Windermere, target arrival around 3:00 PM.
  • Check in to Lakes Hotel and Spa.
  • Light walk along Bowness waterfront.
  • Spa / hot tub / easy dinner.

Jul 25 — Wansfell Pike & Troutbeck Circular

  • Distance: about 7.7 miles
  • Elevation Gain: about 1,922 ft
  • Logistics: Walk to Bowness Interchange, take 599 bus north to Ambleside.
  • Route: Start behind the Salutation Hotel in Ambleside, climb Wansfell Pike, continue toward Troutbeck, return via Jenkins Crag / Waterhead area.
  • Post-hike: The Mortal Man pub in Troutbeck, then 599 bus back toward Bowness.

Jul 26 — Fairfield Horseshoe

  • Distance: about 10.2 miles
  • Elevation Gain: about 3,116 ft
  • Logistics: Take the 555 bus from Windermere / Bowness north to Ambleside.
  • Route: Anti-clockwise loop over Nab Scar, Great Rigg, Fairfield, and the ridges around the horseshoe.
  • Difficulty: Hard mountain day; exposed ridges, rocky footing, weather-sensitive.
  • Post-hike: The Golden Rule in Ambleside, then bus back to the hotel spa.

Jul 27 — Catbells Ridge & Derwentwater Shoreline

  • Distance: about 6.5 to 9 miles depending on extension
  • Elevation Gain: about 1,450 ft
  • Logistics: 555 bus north to Keswick, walk to Keswick Launch, ferry to Hawes End Pier.
  • Route: Ascend Catbells ridge, descend south, return via lakeshore paths.
  • Post-hike: Food and beer in Keswick, then scenic bus ride south.

Jul 28 — Orrest Head Morning Stretch

  • Distance: about 3 miles
  • Purpose: Short final viewpoint walk before leaving for Edinburgh.
Mountain safety: Treat Fairfield Horseshoe as a serious mountain day. Carry waterproof layers, warm layer, offline map, battery backup, water, food, and a turnaround plan if weather deteriorates.
